# codify

Three lessons handed over at once, each testing a different part of codify's
judgement:
1. A reproducible lint gap: fix it in lint with a regression fixture, not with wording.
2. One failure in four eval runs, not reproduced: log a candidate and change nothing.
3. A rule that applies to every skill: propose the kernel diff and wait for sign-off.
